# Support Escalation

## Tiers
P1 pages the on-call engineer immediately. P2 gets a response within 4 hours.
P3 is handled next business day. Severity is set by customer impact, not by
who reports it.

## Current volume
Current open ticket volume is approximately 10 across all queues. Staffing is
planned against this estimate; the ticketing database holds the exact count
and is the source of truth for numbers.

## Handoff
When a ticket moves tiers, the new owner gets the full thread plus a one-line
summary. No ticket changes hands without an explicit accept.
