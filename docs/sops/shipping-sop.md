# Shipping SOP

## Carriers
Standard parcels go by ground carrier; expedited orders use air freight.
Carrier choice is recorded on the shipment record for traceability.

## SLA
Orders ship within 2 business days of payment confirmation. Backordered SKUs
ship when stock arrives and the customer is notified of the new date.

## Exceptions
Weather disruptions and carrier capacity limits pause the SLA clock. The
support team proactively emails affected customers with revised dates.
