# Returns Policy

## Eligibility
Customers may return unused products within 30 days of delivery. Items must be
in original packaging with tags attached. Final-sale items are excluded.

## Process
To start a return, contact support with the order number and reason. Support
issues a return label within one business day and the warehouse inspects the
item on arrival.

## Refunds
Refunds go to the original payment method within 5 business days of inspection.
Partial refunds apply when packaging is missing or the item shows light use.
